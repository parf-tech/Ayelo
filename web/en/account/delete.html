Delete my account

Your profile is hidden immediately and you are signed out of all your devices. Your personal data is anonymised after 30 days.

Way 1: in the application

  1. Open the application and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with your password.

Way 2: from this page, without the application

Enter the e-mail address of your account. We send you a confirmation link, valid for 60 minutes. Deletion is requested only when you open that link and confirm. The answer is the same whether or not the address matches an account.

Service address in this form: [TO BE COMPLETED: to be set by the operator, see docs/runbooks/site-public.md]. For an account created only with Google or Apple (no password), way 2 is not yet available in every case: write to support.

30-day delay

  • For 30 days your account is pending deletion: profile invisible, sessions closed.
  • After that, anonymisation is automatic and final.
  • To change your mind before the end of the delay: contact support [TO BE COMPLETED: cancellation procedure to be confirmed: not verified in the code].

Premium subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play settings, otherwise billing continues.

What is deleted

At anonymisation (after 30 days):

  • E-mail, password, phone number if any, devices and notification tokens, social sign-in identities.
  • Profile, preferences, photos and related files, verification selfie, verification requests.
  • The content of the messages you sent (the conversation stays visible to the other person, marked “deleted account”).
  • Your likes and passes, your blocks, your calls, your stories and invitations, your help requests and appeals, your export archives.
  • The IP address attached to your consents.
  • Raw purchase data received from the subscription provider that contains your identifier.
