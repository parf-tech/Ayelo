Protections from the first message

What the app puts in place, and where it stops.

Verified profiles

A badge means a photo review was done. It is not a guarantee of identity: we do not check identity documents, so stay alert.

Report and block

From a card, a profile or a conversation, open the menu and choose “Report”. You can also block the person.

A reminder about scams

Never send money to someone you met online. The app reminds you.

18+

Adults only

The service is reserved for people aged 18 and over. Your age is asked before the account is created; we do not ask for an identity document.

Three steps

  1. Create your profile

    Give your age, a few details about you and what you are looking for: marriage, a lasting relationship, a fling, friendship or “let’s see”.

  2. Discover profiles

    Refine by country of origin, country of residence and intention. You can report or block at any time.

  3. Chat

    When the interest is mutual, it is a match: you can write to each other.

Everyone is looking for something different

You give your gender and the one you are looking for, then what matters to you. You can also refine by country of origin and country of residence.

Frequently asked questions

How old do I have to be?

18 and over. Your age is asked before the account is created. We do not ask for an identity document.

What does “verified profile” mean?

A photo review was done on this profile. It is not a guarantee of identity: never send money to someone you met online. Safety and reporting

How do I report someone?

From a card, a profile or a conversation, open the menu and choose “Report”. You can also block the person. Community rules

How do I delete my account?

In the app, or from the “Delete my account” page. The profile is hidden right away, then the data is anonymised after 30 days. Delete my account
