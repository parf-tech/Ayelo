Safety and reporting

Your safety starts with a few simple habits. You also have tools in the application.

In immediate danger, contact your country’s emergency services before anything else. This service is not an emergency service.

What we do not do

We do not verify the identity of members. The “verified” badge means a photo review was done, not that identity is guaranteed. Age is checked by declaration. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted (see the privacy policy).

Protect yourself

  • Never send money to someone you met online: transfer, prepaid card, cryptocurrency, “fees”, “emergency”.
  • Be wary of profiles that want to leave the application very quickly, avoid video calls or invent an emergency.
  • Do not share your address, your workplace or identity documents.
  • For a first meeting: public place, someone you trust informed, video call beforehand, your own transport.

Block

From a profile or a conversation, “Block” cuts all contact immediately. You will find the list in Settings, “Blocked people”.

Report

From a profile, a conversation or a call, “Report” opens a short form. Reasons: request for money, harassment or threats, inappropriate behaviour during a meeting, fake profile or spam, person who seems to be a minor, other. You can add free text.

What happens after a report

  1. A team member reviews the report. Target processing time: [TO BE COMPLETED: time to be confirmed by the owner, no commitment made so far].
  2. Depending on severity: warning, temporary suspension, ban, report to the authorities.
  3. The sanctioned member is told the reason and can appeal once.
  4. A person suspected of being a minor is, in some cases, suspended as a precaution, then the case is reviewed as a priority.

Retention of report text: see the privacy policy. We do not tell the reported person who reported them.

Unlawful content or minors
