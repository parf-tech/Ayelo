Community rules

Draft 0.1, not legally validated. These rules apply to every member and complete the terms of use. This English text is a working translation of the French draft.

What we expect

  • Be yourself: your photos must show you, and be recent.
  • Be respectful: no insults, threats, harassment or hate speech.
  • Respect consent: “no” means “no”. No sexual content sent without agreement.
  • Protect your privacy and that of others: do not share someone’s contact details without their agreement.

What is forbidden

  • Any request or offer of money, gifts, prepaid cards, transfers, cryptocurrency or “fees” (romance scams).
  • Fake profiles, identity theft, multiple profiles.
  • Any content involving minors, human trafficking, sexual exploitation, fraud: zero tolerance, reported to the authorities.
  • Commercial solicitation, spam, links to paid services.
  • Using bots or harvesting data from the service.

Report, block

From a profile, a conversation or a call: “Block” or “Report”. Reasons offered: request for money, harassment, behaviour during a meeting, fake profile or spam, person who seems to be a minor, other. Every report is reviewed. More detail: safety and reporting.

What can happen

Warning, temporary suspension, permanent ban, report to the authorities. You are told the reason and can appeal once.

Tips for meeting

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Tell someone you trust.
  • Keep the conversation in the application until you trust the person.
  • Never send money.
  • Use a video call before meeting.
