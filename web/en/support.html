Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

In the application or from the Delete my account page. 30-day delay.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

In the App Store or Google Play settings. Deleting the application or the account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In the application: Settings, “Export my data”. One request per day; the archive stays available for 7 days.

How do I report or block someone?

See safety and reporting.

I was sanctioned, what can I do?

In the application: Settings, “Sanctions and appeals”. You can appeal once.

I can no longer access my account

Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen. If that is not enough, write to us from the account’s e-mail address.
